Recent Changes

Published updates.

November 8, 2026

  • The life-support bar now uses the same rounded-down whole days as its label: 27.x days displays 27 out of a 28-day target. Actual supplies, consumption and refill calculations retain their precision.

November 8, 2026

  • Luxury cabins, people and manual passenger bookings are supported. Luxury uses the campaign convention of Cr1,000 per cabin and Cr5,000 per person per 28 days. Singularity's room-based fares are provided as a reference for agreed booking terms.
  • Life-support refills combine cabin and passenger-service costs into one daily rate, rounded up to a whole Credit before calculating the purchased duration. Separate cabin/support payments are retired. Existing stock and historical receipts are preserved without automatic credits or catch-up charges.
  • Use station life support is a per-ship switch while docked at a port. It pauses onboard consumption, defaults off, and switches off automatically on a jump. An empty ship uses no cabin LSS.
  • The Cr100 information popup explains the allowance for small taxes, incidental expenses and tips. Mortgage and maintenance cash debits follow credit rounding too; their exact scheduled payments remain unchanged and receipts identify the incidental difference.

November 8, 2026

  • A compact Ship dropdown and Add ship action support independent campaigns, with separate cargo, credits, location, route, settings and history. Release #230
  • Backup all ships saves the whole group; Export this ship saves only the selected campaign. Load clearly distinguishes replacing the selected ship from replacing all ships, with a backup acknowledgement before either action. Older single-ship files remain supported.
  • Existing browser saves are preserved, and ship-switching guards help prevent unfinished edits from being lost.
  • Delete ship removes the selected campaign after a clear confirmation and backup acknowledgement, preserving the other ships. At least one ship must remain; Reset campaign can clear the final ship.

October 26, 2026

  • Separate tool changelogs with contact and feedback details, linked below the main launch buttons. Release #186

October 23, 2026

  • Monthly maintenance information stays beside its action, including on narrow screens. Release #133
  • Read-only accounting date ranges and private PDF preview. Release #132

October 22, 2026

  • Backup and Load shortcuts beside Copy Player Link. Release #131
  • More compact Sell rows with aligned net proceeds. Release #130
