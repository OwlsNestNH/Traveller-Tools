Crow owns the commentary — candidate under review

Engine-offered Companion careers for native Aslan and Vargr, and Core careers for Imperial-upbringing Aslan and Vargr, retain the shared dark career screens, pictured Crow guidance, information controls and visible recorded gold dice. Career record consistency — candidate under review

Career Characteristics and Skills use the same exclusive expansion behavior as the accepted record bars: opening either closes the other. Keyboard controls, character contents, recorded dice, career choices, saves and history are unchanged.

Existing editors and identity facts are retained. Rules, choices, recorded results, saves and navigation are unchanged. This candidate has not been published.

Companion and psionic screen theme

Companion admission, education skill choices and standalone psionic training reuse the accepted navy, gold and cyan theme. Source text within Crow’s dark panel uses the readable light text color. Existing fields, optional choices, recorded results, rules and saved characters are unchanged.

Career result clarity

Ageing guidance names the pending ageing roll. Undercover checks show their recorded modifier, final total, target and outcome alongside the gold dice. Career event narratives and actual awards stay on the main screen, with table alternatives and source bookkeeping in the existing circled-i information popup. Rules, accepted dice, full logs and saved characters are unchanged.

Vargr career screens — candidate under review

Native Vargr careers use the shared career popup with specialty requirements, concise training labels, source information and pictured Crow guidance. Loner, Charisma, psionic eligibility and source-specific exceptions retain their existing rules. Imperial upbringing stays separate. New revision .35 offers Core Drifter, Loner or an eligible Vargr draft after failed qualification; draft destinations require an explicit recorded referee interpretation, with no automatic Marine substitution. Existing saves retain their original rules. This candidate has not been published.

Player-facing event descriptions and University skill choices

Education events now retain their available story, named event and consequences before and after the roll, without raw code in the popup. Both University skill boxes are shown together. Additional Careers Cancel returns to the standard choices. The wider desktop sidebar has three-across statistics, a dedicated Aslan origin bar and always-visible identity/age/terms/cash/debt. Rules, recorded dice and saves are unchanged.

Beta coverage and limitations

This is a source-assisted beta, not a complete, fully automated or independently rules-certified generator. Keep the relevant books and your referee available. Source references identify the rules being used, but generic fallback references may point to a career's opening page rather than the precise event page.

  • Manual consequences stay manual. A generic source step applies only supported immediate effects you enter. A confirmed note does not install future modifiers, permissions, transfers or deferred awards. Track unsupported consequences separately with your referee; do not treat confirmation as automation.
  • Unusual Vargr psionic-testing opportunities and exceptional Aslan outcomes involving unprinted parcel sizes or unrecorded property still need source adjudication.
  • Specialist kinship, Brotherhood, Secret School and standalone Land Parcel benefits; Sainthood and repeated Patronage; some ship, implant and equipment awards are not fully automated. Inheritance, corporate holdings, land income and unpriced equipment also remain bounded or manual.
  • Cross-book career and education eligibility, ambiguous cultural rules, previous or unprinted outcomes, Aezorgh social-statistic changes and unusual medical funding need referee rulings. Optional characteristic displays do not implement every rule that uses them.
  • Optional anagathics, post-career shopping and campaign group-skill packages are not fully automated. These are concrete limits, not a claim that every possible source exception has been identified.

Save compatibility and privacy

Export backups before updates or starting another character. Older or otherwise incompatible rules revisions open only as unverified, read-only recorded history, with an unchanged-original download. They cannot continue under new rules or become a validated character sheet. There is no automatic migration. Deferred Zhodani histories are also kept read only without changing their bytes.

The mandatory local log is not cryptographic proof or protection against someone editing their own file. No account or cloud save is provided. Names, notes and campaign data stay in your browser or local API. The published site uses the existing shared Hits visit counter for the canonical tool path only; it does not send your character, query or fragment. Counting failures do not affect generation.
