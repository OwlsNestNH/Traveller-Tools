Recent changes

November 10, 2026 · Cancel character creation

  • Cancel beside the step actions opens a centered airlock confirmation. Keep character or Escape preserves your progress; Airlock character starts fresh on the first page. Exported files, unrelated saved records and protected unreadable drafts remain untouched.

  • The full beta notice now opens from the small Beta 0.1 informational in the header. The centered window keeps every existing limitation and referee note, with Close and Escape controls, keyboard focus return and narrow-screen scrolling.
  • Character rules, choices, saves and logs are unchanged.

November 10, 2026 · Optional flat starting Luck

  • GM Controls → House Rules now offers Start Luck at 6, off by default. When enabled before rolling, selected or later-added Luck starts at 6 without a roll. Checking this house rule also selects the optional Luck characteristic. Turning the rule off leaves Luck selected, using normal 2D.
  • Normal 2D Luck, other characteristic rules and existing saved values are unchanged. The log records the chosen house rule; older histories replay without changes.

November 9, 2026 · Equipment details and bounded event outcomes

  • New characters retain ordinary equipment and artefacts when awarded. Individual finalization buttons collect details later, preserving separate awards and linked improvement choices. Artefact descriptions may remain unknown; no powers or value are invented.
  • Bounded Aslan events now offer direct defence and territory checks, optional next-term career permission, retained conditional Clan Shares and recorded land or restricted equipment-credit entitlements. Exceptional unrecorded property still needs source review.
  • New-rules benefit rolls preserve real printed row 8 and cap higher results at the actual last printed row. Female Guardian choices exclude Independence while retaining the other legal choices.
  • Recorded player rulings handle unpayable Believer false-teachings reductions and Human/Vargr psionic acquisition retries with accumulated penalties. Existing Core service training permissions remain available.
  • Revision .21 preserves compatible .16 through .20 histories without silently changing their mechanics. Released species and career scope is unchanged; Zhodani creation remains deferred.

November 9, 2026 · Event context, specialties and Academy option

  • Event results now show concise descriptions, recorded dice, table outcomes and actual character changes, including pre-career events and nested mishaps. Choices retain their event context, and the full log keeps these details available. Source-assisted consequences remain explicit.
  • New characters choose required specialties when skills are received, including University level-1 awards. Level-0 awards remain unspecialized. Existing .18 characters keep their saved deferred-award workflow; imports do not silently replace their rules.
  • An optional GM setting, off by default, adds one Service Skills roll in the first lifetime career immediately after a completed matching Army, Marine or Navy Academy. It does not replace ordinary level-0 basic training or change Academy graduation awards.
  • The released species and career scope is unchanged. These improvements do not certify complete rules coverage.

November 9, 2026 · Career and skill corrections

  • In historical .18 characters, ordinary unspecified skill specialties can be allocated later, including at finalization. Printed named or restricted specialties still resolve when awarded. Separate awards can be split between specialties, with an optional early-allocation control.
  • Connections preserve award order and their level-three limit. Open Science, Language and Profession subjects use consistent case-insensitive identities in new characters.
  • Corrected forced Drifter drafts, benefit forfeiture, Scout departure timing and Believer event promotion. Believer buyouts now offer refusal or either recorded payment calculation at the event, with forfeiture and totals shown. University and Academy award prompts now identify their context more clearly.
  • Recorded pre-career interpretations are applied to new characters. The .18 release preserved compatible .16/.17 histories without silently applying new mechanics to their saved events.
  • Released species remain Human, Aslan, Vargr and Aezorgh. Zhodani development remains separate and unavailable in this release.

November 9, 2026 · Clearer character results

  • Training choices include small circled-i popups showing skills and characteristic bumps in table order. Roll results name the received skill or the choice still needed. Speciality suggestions stay within the awarded base skill.
  • A shared result panel identifies rolls and applied effects across the released species and careers, including life events, injuries, contacts, allies and advancement. Pending choices and source decisions remain explicit. Current career, assignment and rank appear prominently at the top of the character record.
  • Prisoner escape now explains the opportunity and lets you choose Stealth or Deception for the 10+ check. Later-career basic training also explains why a regular training roll follows.
  • The .17 release introduced these escape controls while preserving .16 characters and their original actions and past escape results. Continuing with the new escape command requires this updated app.

November 9, 2026 · Beta 0.1: source-assisted character generation

  • Build a Human, Aslan, Vargr or Aezorgh Traveller through characteristic setup, background, education and career choices, with a live character sheet. The release offers 36 career entries; a listed career does not mean every event, mishap or benefit is automated. Zhodani species, careers and their Intendant/Noble PSI option are deferred.
  • Implemented paths include ordinary training, career checks, rank awards, benefits and ageing, plus bounded source-specific choices. Human optional PSI, Core/Vargr Psion and Companion paths remain available, with source decisions where needed.
  • Optional boons, score placement/swapping, minimum-total rerolls and extra characteristics are labelled. The Aezorgh six-2D6 method is an optional house rule, not the published default.
  • Rolls, choices, corrections and restored steps remain in the always-on action log. GM changes require a reason and preserve the original record. Save character & log exports a JSON backup; Print character includes the record and saved notes.
  • The browser keeps one local draft. Load validates a current or explicitly compatible rules save before replacing the draft. At a source stop, you can export the pending character and log before deciding how to proceed.

Beta coverage and limitations

This is a source-assisted beta, not a complete, fully automated or independently rules-certified generator. Keep the relevant books and your referee available. Source references identify the rules being used, but generic fallback references may point to a career's opening page rather than the precise event page.

  • Manual consequences stay manual. A generic source step applies only supported immediate effects you enter. A confirmed note does not install future modifiers, permissions, transfers or deferred awards. Track unsupported consequences separately with your referee; do not treat confirmation as automation.
  • Unusual Vargr psionic-testing opportunities and exceptional Aslan outcomes involving unprinted parcel sizes or unrecorded property still need source adjudication.
  • Specialist kinship, Brotherhood, Secret School and standalone Land Parcel benefits; Sainthood and repeated Patronage; some ship, implant and equipment awards are not fully automated. Inheritance, corporate holdings, land income and unpriced equipment also remain bounded or manual.
  • Cross-book career and education eligibility, ambiguous cultural rules, previous or unprinted outcomes, Aezorgh social-statistic changes and unusual medical funding need referee rulings. Optional characteristic displays do not implement every rule that uses them.
  • Optional anagathics, post-career shopping and campaign group-skill packages are not fully automated. These are concrete limits, not a claim that every possible source exception has been identified.

Save compatibility and privacy

Export backups before updates or starting another character. Older or otherwise incompatible rules revisions open only as unverified, read-only recorded history, with an unchanged-original download. They cannot continue under new rules or become a validated character sheet. There is no automatic migration. Deferred Zhodani histories are also kept read only without changing their bytes.

The mandatory local log is not cryptographic proof or protection against someone editing their own file. No account or cloud save is provided. Names, notes and campaign data stay in your browser or local API. The published site uses the existing shared Hits visit counter for the canonical tool path only; it does not send your character, query or fragment. Counting failures do not affect generation.
