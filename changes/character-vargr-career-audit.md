# Vargr career candidate: source boundaries

The presentation candidate covers the nine native Vargr careers and 27 specialties in *Aliens of Charted Space Volume 1 Update 2026*, printed pp. 188–205. General creation is on pp. 186–187; shared events and benefits are on pp. 206–207. Imperial upbringing remains a separate Core-career route. Aezorgh is outside this update.

## Verified rules and current gaps

- Page 186 explicitly permits Core Drifter (with its Core p. 28 reference), Loner or the Vargr draft after failed qualification. New revision .35 exposes all three alternatives, preserving eligibility and one recorded draft die. Compatible .34 and earlier saves retain their original behavior without automatic migration.
- Loner lasts one term. Native Vargr do not take a qualification penalty for previous careers.
- The draft has printed outcome weights, but its destination labels do not explicitly identify revised career IDs or assignments. The revised Vargr profiles have no standalone Marine career.
- Military/Army and Spacer/Navy have matching assignment names. Military/Raider is described on p. 198 as a shipboard fighting role, making it a plausible Marine interpretation, not an explicit mapping instruction.
- Existing Citizen Worker, Merchant and Scientist summary/detail discrepancies retain the engine's documented detailed-page interpretations.

## Mapping options: no default selected

1. **Literal Core destinations.** Use Core profiles corresponding to the printed career names. This uses Core skills, ranks, benefits and any applicable cross-species characteristic procedures. The book does not explicitly authorize this interpretation for each native Vargr draft result.
2. **Native equivalents.** Use native Agent, Military/Army, Military/Raider and Spacer/Navy. This retains native Charisma-based progression and benefits. The Raider substitution requires an explicit interpretation; it is not verified RAW.
3. **Recorded source/referee resolution.** Record the printed draft die and result once, then obtain an explicit career/assignment mapping and reason before entry. This avoids choosing an undocumented global interpretation but adds a source-resolution step. Cancel/reload must preserve the die; the procedure must not permit rerolls or unrelated career access.

Any implementation must preserve source-authorized Drifter and Loner alternatives, ordinary draft-use restrictions and existing saves. New mechanics or player-driven nested-roll checkpoints require a separate rules-revision gate and substantial replay/continuation coverage. Old histories must retain their original behavior.

## Implemented referee-resolution policy

The reviewed candidate uses option 3: the recorded draft result opens a bounded destination list, with no default, and requires a referee confirmation plus source/reason. Marine can be interpreted as native Military/Raider or a Core Marine specialty only through that explicit ruling; neither is asserted as corrected RAW. Closing or reloading retains the die. Drifter and Loner each offer their three specialties without a qualification roll. The separate forced Core draft from Drifter event 11 keeps its original destination and redraft permission.

A synthetic 52-action .34 replay and its next continuation match the deployed baseline in state, logs, RNG and exported bytes. No personal character file is included.
