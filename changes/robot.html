Recent changes

November 8, 2026 · Beta 0.2: reference sheet

  • Choose Reference sheet beside the file controls for a compact, light-background preview with Robot, Hits, Locomotion, Speed, TL and Cost, followed by skills, attacks, manipulators, endurance, traits, programming and options.
  • Print or save the selected format as PDF from your browser. Long lists continue onto later pages; unresolved values, provisional assumptions and component ownership stay labelled.
  • Detailed statblock remains the default and retains complete installation and source details. Format selection leaves design saves and JSON backups unchanged. No reference artwork is included.

November 8, 2026 · Beta 0.1: new-build construction

  • Six independent construction stages cover chassis, locomotion, physical options, robot brains, skill packages and finalisation, with live cost, Slots, Bandwidth and construction checks.
  • Ordinary robot construction sits alongside scoped microbot, nanorobot, android, biological, cybernetic, avatar and clone paths, plus robot and brain integration with external hosts.
  • Supported equipment, weapon installations, ammunition and software retain source references, selected figures and fitting conditions. Separate installations and linked weapon copies keep their own settings.
  • Save, Load and Save a copy keep named designs in this browser. Export and import JSON backups, or print a readable statblock. Import opens an unsaved draft; it does not replace a saved design.
  • Construction checks, unresolved choices and referee conventions remain labelled in the editor, review and printable statblock. Metamorphic: provisional and optional external-wear accounting retain their explicit conventions.

Beta coverage

Broad new-build construction is supported within the implemented rules. A checked design does not certify every legal Robot Handbook design.

  • Host legality not checked. Vehicle and ship capacity, links and host records remain separate. Recipient record required applies to relevant biological and cybernetic paths.
  • Acquisition only entries retain source prices without inventing missing brain ratings, implant fit or production limits. Some movement choices, upgrade prices and higher-tech effects still need a source-supported or referee answer.
  • Selected external equipment comes from the Central Supply Catalogue, Vehicle Handbook and High Guard. These are bounded selections, not complete external catalogues.
  • Combat, treatment, maintenance and factory operation remain separate workflows. Construction estimates do not resolve their outcomes.
