Calculation assumptions

Transit starts from rest, using constant acceleration or symmetric flip-and-burn braking. The calculator uses 150,000,000 km per AU and 8.3 minutes of light time per AU.

100D clearance is advisory and is not added to the flown route. Communication delay does not model blockage or relays. The inherited World Builder's Handbook orbit table and Astrogation rules were not independently source-verified by these updates.
