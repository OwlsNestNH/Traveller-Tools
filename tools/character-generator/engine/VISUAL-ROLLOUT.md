## Remaining staged audits

- Dedicated statistic/PSI/manual-dice/source-ruling information dialogs: inventory
  their actual prompts, portraits, source copy and theme individually. The shared
  opening-help conversion does not establish that all of them are finished.
- Prisoner and Psion exceptional routes: obtain permitted-route desktop/mobile
  evidence without bypassing eligibility, source support or referee permission.
- Existing Aslan family/background popups retain their dark/gold dice design;
  in-popup Crow coverage is not uniform. Native alien career refurbishment stays
  separate from this Imperial rollout.
- Vargr/Aezorgh career work and any Corsair/Pirate artwork exception remain later
  stages. The suggested small skull-and-crossbones flag is only a future concept.
- Centred-sheet/navigation redesign remains deferred. No completion/help change
  authorizes a navigation redesign or release.

All release candidates require exact-head checks, independent review and the
agreed user visual review. Existing saves, in-memory choices, accepted results,
Back/Cancel behavior and mechanical rules must remain intact.

help headings keep whole words with Close on its own persistent row. A verified
deceased state receives a short failure outcome; all original recorded details
remain in the closed Result details disclosure and in the unchanged full log.

Retirement previews end on an ordinary accepted benefit command, not GM replay.
Actual recorded benefit dice are shown; current Clan Share awards and unspent
balances are distinguished. The sheet labels aggregate holdings separately from
individual award rows. This is a display clarification, not an accounting repair
or save migration.
