# Shared career checkpoints

Revision `character-preview-2026-11-13.32` separates supported Core career event/mishap consequence dice from the already accepted table roll. The read-only checkpoint helper inspects the pending effect and its career/term context; the existing guarded roll command authorizes exactly that pending step. Borrowed-career routes retain their own context validation. Missing characteristics retain source resolution. The existing revision gates preserve earlier saved histories and their RNG sequence; no save is silently rewritten or repaired.

The requested interpretation of later Core career entry is one chosen basic-training skill followed by survival, without another ordinary training roll at that boundary. Successful advancement still earns its separate roll. First-career training, Citizen/Drifter assignment-table training and the explicit academy training option retain their separate rules. Native alien training is unchanged.

Core Update 2022 p.18 limits the explicit “instead of rolling” phrase to the first career and describes later basic training separately. That wording supports conflicting readings. Publisher-hosted community discussion also records the disagreement; it is not an official erratum or a definitive ruling: <https://forum.mongoosepublishing.com/threads/basic-training-confusion.124173/>. The requested tool behavior must therefore be presented as the selected interpretation, not an undisputed correction to RAW. No definitive official clarification was found in the checked sources.

Required skill awards redirected to manual source resolution now require a written referee ruling and explicit confirmation of replacement/omission. Normal skill selection remains available. A known award is not silently granted, erased or assigned an invented specialization. Historical manual rulings are preserved.

Equipment completion remains a checklist, not another benefit roll. Core p.47 governs actual item selection and award limits; recorded entitlements survive Back/Cancel and reload. The UI explains why Finish is unavailable while details remain pending.

Verification uses synthetic characters and includes cross-career checks, sequential consequence dice, stale/repeated roll authorization, required award review, basic-training/advancement distinction and historical revision boundaries. Private player logs and source PDFs must never be committed or uploaded as public fixtures.
