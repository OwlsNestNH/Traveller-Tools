# Imperial career presentation

The shared popup registry covers the 14 existing Core careers and the two
optional Companion careers. It projects specialty names, concise skill names
and current-rank references from the existing catalog. It does not duplicate
qualification rules, full skill tables, rank effects, events or benefits.

## References and exceptions

Core Rulebook Update 2022 career spreads: Agent 22–23, Army 24–25,
Citizen 26–27, Drifter 28–29, Entertainer 30–31, Marine 32–33,
Merchant 34–35, Navy 36–37, Noble 38–39, Rogue 40–41,
Scholar 42–43, Scout 44–45, Prisoner 56–57 and Psion 236–237.
General basic training is on p.18; Psion training and permission exceptions
are on p.229. Companion Update 2024: Truther 36–37; Believer 38–40
(mishaps 39, events 40).

- Citizen and Drifter use assignment basic training. Psion uses its specialist
  table, and Believer its specialism table. Other careers retain their catalog
  training source. Earned advancement training remains distinct.
- Military officer/enlisted tracks and civilian assignment-specific ranks stay
  separate. Scout retains its actual ranks. Truther has Following rather than
  a conventional advancement/rank ladder.
- Prisoner retains forced entry and its Parole Threshold/release procedure.
  Psion retains referee permission, talent acquisition and qualification when
  changing specialty. The popup is presentation, not authorization.
- Native career-specific event, mishap, benefit and equipment choices remain in
  the engine; the shared shell presents the current typed state.

## Recorded results and saves

Result comments use deterministic cosmetic selection, never generation RNG.
Success/failure and exceptional-success comments require validated check
results. Fictional failures remain distinct from factual technical errors.
Recorded gold dice show accepted faces, modifiers and targets when evidence is
available; comments do not create mechanical effects.

Revision `.33` adds explicit pending consequence dice to native Companion
career events/mishaps using the same context-checked actions as Core events.
The same revision extends the selected later-career basic-only interpretation to
these two optional Imperial careers; earned advancement training stays separate.
This is the previously selected interpretation of the ambiguous Core p.18
wording, not a claim of a new official clarification. Revision `.32` keeps its
prior replay behavior. Existing session revisions and
accepted outcomes are retained; importing a save does not upgrade its rules or
retroactively reroll an event.

## Review status

This is an in-progress rollout. Selection/source-popup browser checks cover
11 ordinary Core careers at desktop and phone widths. Exceptional routes and
complete term/benefit flows require their dedicated checks before release.
Navy is the first staged visual preview. No publication is implied by a
registry entry or an individual passing presentation check.

## Controlled presentation extension

The pictured Crow component retains existing instruction nodes and controls.
Career result comments and the next instruction share one pictured speaker;
required-action warnings replace the normal speaker within their active scope.
Technical errors keep their existing factual default-Crow dialog.

Core academy training, event prompts and graduation previews use the Education
shell. Preview numbers come from the existing graduation calculation; the
academy source is Core p.16 (INT 7+, honours 11+, with the listed END/SOC
bonuses). The presentation distinguishes those bonuses from the INT modifier.
Acknowledgement wording describes the preceding action without suggesting
that a later offered event roll is forbidden. Original recorded logs remain.

Character-sheet history groups by recorded Pre-Term/Term timing, retaining
individual timing labels and every current-branch entry. Unknown timing stays
explicit. Print temporarily expands groups and restores their previous state.

The sheet-centred layout study under `verification/imperial-layout-concept*`
is deferred at the owner's request. It is not run by CI or used by application
navigation. Existing Education/Background screen layout remains in place.

The owner approved the clearer outfit comparison at head 091c98b. The exact
shown lighter images now occupy the existing technician/professor/referee asset
paths. Default Crow (`crow-pet.png`), image selection, screen layout and technical
error behavior are unchanged. Original costumes are retained under
`verification/review-assets/*-before.png`; the comparison renders previous and
approved artwork at the same 112 × 128 phone portrait size.

Military Crow remains a review-only draft under `verification/review-assets`; it
has no production image mapping pending owner visual approval.
