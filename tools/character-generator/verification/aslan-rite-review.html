      About your Rite of Passage

      Your ordinary background skills are chosen first. The rite is a separate, single 2D roll.

      How RITE is calculated

      1. Add the two dice.
      2. Compare the total with STR, DEX, END, INT, EDU and SOC.
      3. Count each score that is strictly greater than the total. Equal or lower scores do not count.

      The count is your RITE modifier. Use it directly for checks that call for RITE; it is not converted to an ordinary characteristic DM.

      What if the dice match?

      Doubles also trigger the corresponding rite event from the source. Calculate RITE first, then resolve the event and any required follow-up before continuing.

      Only matching dice trigger that event. This preview uses 3 and 4, so there is no additional event or reward.

      What comes next?

      The completed rite leads to pre-career education choices. Education is optional: “Go straight to careers” opens career selection instead. The rite itself does not enrol you in education or enter a career.
