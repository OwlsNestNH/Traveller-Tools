About your pride

A pride is a group of families within your clan, functioning much like a political party or regional government.

Your pride is determined separately from your clan. Roll two dice, add them together and use the total below.

Pride roll · 2D

  • 2–4: Major pride
  • 5–9: Average pride
  • 10–12: Minor pride

Giving your clan a name

You may name your clan before rolling pride, or leave it unnamed. Naming does not reroll your clan or change its type or scope.
