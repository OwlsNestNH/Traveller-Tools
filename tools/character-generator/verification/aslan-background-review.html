About background skills

How many do I choose?

Your ordinary background allowance is 3 + your EDU modifier, with a minimum of 0. Each chosen skill starts at level 0.

What about my family skill?

Family awards are additional. They do not use up your ordinary background choices. Choose distinct new skills; an already awarded skill is not offered again.

Why these choices?

There is no roll on this screen. Select your own skills, then accept them when your selection is complete.

What comes next?

The Rite of Passage follows your ordinary background choices. Its roll is a separate step.
