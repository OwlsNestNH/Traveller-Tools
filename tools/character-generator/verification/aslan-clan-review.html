Aslan · Family and heritage

A family belongs to a clan.
Where do your roots reach?

Roll 2D to discover your clan type.

Your dice, clan type and scope will appear here.

Some clan types need one extra die for their scope.
It is rolled only if your 2D result requires it.

When is the scope die rolled?
  • 3 or 11 · Major Vassal: 1–3 sectors. Roll 1D, halve and round up (D3).
  • 4 or 10 · Multiworld: 1–6 sectors. Use the 1D result.
  • 5 · Vassal: 1–2 sectors. Roll 1D, divide by three and round up (D2).

All other clan results have a fixed scope. No additional die is rolled.

Referee-selected clan instead

Your referee may choose your clan instead of rolling. With GM changes enabled, record its name, scope and source basis.

GM changes are off in this design example. Choosing a clan would not roll any dice.
