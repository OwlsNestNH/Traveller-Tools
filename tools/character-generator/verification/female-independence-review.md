# Female Aslan Independence reroll audit

User ruling, 2026-11-09: "If a female gets independence re-roll". Applies to new .18 sessions, preserving .16/.17.

Source references: Aliens of Charted Space Vol 1 Update 2026 p17 family table; pp19–20 distinguish cultural associations from the real male-only Independence restriction. This change implements the user's reroll ruling, not an assertion that the book explicitly prints that reroll.

All expansion training and material tables were inspected. Mandatory female results: Outcast family row4 and Wanderer material row4. Wanderer is normally male-only; the latter is defensive handling of an existing table without expanding career eligibility. Other Independence entries are printed gender substitutions (retained) or choices with legal alternatives (retained with stable indices). No acquisition check is rolled for the rejected female result.

The same material helper handles repeated borrowed-ship replacements. Source-training effects are limited to Core Navy/Vargr Citizen tables without Independence; psionic replacement is a separate Zhodani path. Events and fixed awards have no invented source table. Choice-only Independence is unavailable rather than allowing a forbidden award; a fixed award without a reroll source pauses explicitly.

Every natural die is kept in the action log; family records preserve rejected and accepted rows and source; benefit roll history preserves every natural/modifier/result. 64 attempts bound pathological table loops; an API failure commits no event. Tests cover legacy branches, male acquisition boundaries, repeated family rolls, replacement replay, import, idempotency, benefit modifiers/accounting, source audit, stable choice indices, and the bound.
