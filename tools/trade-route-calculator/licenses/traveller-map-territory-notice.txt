The boundary and sector-footprint walkers in js/map-territory.mjs adapt algorithms
from The Traveller Map. Modified 2026-10-08 for JavaScript/SVG output, an integer
coordinate lattice, buffer-aware stroke segments, validated metadata styles,
and pointer-inert map layers.

The standalone player-map-preview.mjs visual revision (2026-10-19) uses original
SVG primitives following the established label placement and N/S base-symbol
conventions inspected in RenderContext.cs, RenderUtil.cs and Stylesheet.cs.
No map screenshot, font asset, or server-rendered tile is bundled by this revision.
