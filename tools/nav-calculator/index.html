Reasoning: Trans-solar multipliers

The multiplier is navigation geometry + hazard avoidance, not physics. It represents taking a safer arc around the star instead of cutting through.

If you want to ignore stellar hazards, choose Direct transit instead of Trans-solar.

Direct distance uses the two orbital radii and their angular separation (eight-point compass). Trans-solar: (AU1+AU2)×safety. These detour factors are navigation assumptions; communication delay uses direct separation, without modelling signal blockage or relays.
