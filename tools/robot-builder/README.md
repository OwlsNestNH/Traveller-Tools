# Robot Builder (Beta 0.2)

Release label: `0.2.0-beta.1`. This is an application release identifier, independent of the unchanged design JSON schema. See the [Robot Builder changelog](../../changes/robot.html).

The beta supports broad ordinary and special new-build construction, with source references and explicit coverage boundaries. A checked result applies to the selected implemented rules; it does **not** certify every legal Robot Handbook design.

## Release scope

Ordinary chassis, movement, manipulators, equipment, weapons, brains and software sit alongside scoped special construction and acquisition paths. Saved designs, separate installations, component snapshots, JSON backups and printable statblocks retain selected facts and qualifications. Metamorphic construction and external wear keep their explicit versioned referee conventions.

Source omissions, referee choices, external host legality and recipient or biological records remain visible boundaries. Acquisition prices do not establish missing operating ratings, implant fit or production limits. Combat, treatment, maintenance, retrofit histories, manufacturing, fleets and full character careers remain separate workflows. Selected CSC, Vehicle Handbook and High Guard integrations are bounded additions, not complete external catalogues.

The coverage matrix and remaining construction inventory track concrete construction gaps separately from those boundaries. Representative examples and passing tests are evidence for their stated scope, not whole-book certification. Release integration still requires review and the full configured checks on the final candidate.

## Architecture

- `engine/`: deterministic rules, typed catalogue, source references, design schema, validation and calculation. No DOM, browser storage or network dependence.
- `engine/composites.mjs`: source-scoped integration of independently revalidated robot snapshots. Component construction costs are distinct from listed prices and external host costs. A composite robot keeps its own selected list price without replacing combined construction accounting.
- `js/api.mjs`: application API. It composes the engine with explicit named-design saves, import/export and read-only statblock projection. Browser persistence is injected and independently tested.
- `js/app.mjs`: rendering and user interaction only. It imports the API, never the rules modules. Dropdown choices and constraints come from the engine.
- `js/review-model.mjs` projects engine output without DOM or construction calculations; `js/review.mjs` renders that read-only API result. The same projection supplies the printable statblock.
- No server is required for the current adapter. The boundary is a stable programmatic API, not a claim that presentation and rules share one implementation.

The six navigation stages retain the Handbook names: Chassis Size, Locomotion, Physical Options, Robot Brain, Skill Packages, Finalisation. Special construction paths provide their own metadata and rules.

The implementation cites printed pages of **Robot Handbook (Mongoose Publishing, 2022)**. Core characteristic DMs follow **Traveller Core Rulebook Update 2022**, p9. Long-range missile case procurement additionally cites **High Guard Update 2022**, pp 36-37, using the inspected revised printing (copyright 2026). Individual long-range pricing remains unresolved. A bounded forty-three-device miscellaneous-implant subset cites **Central Supply Catalogue Update 2023**, pp 63-65, 73-79, 82-83, 86, 88, 90-91, 105-107, 121, 125-126 and 129-130, from its revised printing (copyright 2026); these edition-specific choices do not replace Core rows. One hundred and thirty-three additional weapon profiles (thirty-one slug, twenty-two energy, twenty-seven heavy, thirty-nine melee, five archaic, two standard-ammunition launchers and seven direct payloads) plus six wearable-only acquisitions and twenty source-specific weapon accessories cite the same CSC printing, pp131-155 and 157-179, with distinct source IDs rather than overwriting Core profiles. Source books and private research files are not included in this repository. The standard deconstruction chamber uses the explicit Robot Handbook p119 installation row. Five fixed-purpose drone/swarm consoles from pp 119 and 122 use their printed masses through the p92 miscellaneous-implant route; they do not become generic computers. The older spreadsheet is not an authoritative source.

The engine catalogue and every evaluation return a current coverage inventory. The [source coverage matrix](verification/source-coverage-matrix.md) and [remaining construction inventory](verification/construction-gap-inventory.md) separate concrete implementation gaps from external dependencies, source ambiguities and contextual operating rules. A result can be invalid, blocked, or checked only within the implemented subset. These states are shown separately from referee-dependent choices. Missing options are not silently represented by zero cost or a generic override.

Current coverage continues to change during implementation. See `getCatalogue(design).coverage` and `evaluateDesign(design).coverage` rather than assuming that a row in a catalogue proves all of its prerequisites and operating rules are implemented.

## Source-defined vehicle options

Fourteen choices from Vehicle Handbook Update 2026 have a separate source catalogue: the decoy, prismatic aerosol and smoke dischargers (pp74-75,77), Light Crane (p68), Basic/Improved External Samplers and Basic/Improved/Advanced Ground Penetrating Radar (p69), four Vehicle Parachute tiers (p72) and allocated-Space UNREP (p89). Robot Handbook p62 supplies the 64-Slots-per-Space conversion. The full Robot Handbook and its construction catalogue were checked for equivalents; no matching construction equivalent was identified for this subset, and related Handbook paths were reviewed. Handbook options retain precedence. Selection stores an authoritative ID and quantity, never editable source prices, TLs or Spaces. Generic external input records remain separate, unverified estimates.

Printed charge capacities are not an included or loaded inventory. Hardware accounting is supported; operating clearance, control interfaces and deployment remain contextual. Additional requested charges retain unresolved price and mass, blocking totals rather than becoming free consumables. No armour, skill or defence bonus is applied automatically. The original six utility installations retain their source hardware TL independently of robot TL. The Light Crane remains its TL1/500kg introduction configuration. Each sampler installation selects one medium; analysis and adjacent-laboratory delivery remain conditional. Radar retains individual nominal depth, environmental/armour limits and conditional detection checks. Advanced radar drones are dedicated device parts, not additional constructed robots or a skill grant. Power context uses the selected robot state without certifying utility readiness or inventing an energy budget. Inapplicable retained charges/media/allocations stay visible for correction and never create phantom purchases. The remaining catalogue, higher-hardware-TL crane scaling, prototype variants and full vehicle-host legality are separate work.

Parachute acquisition uses the selected chassis's immutable printed Vehicle-Space equivalent, not Base Slots divided by 64 or remaining capacity. The printed equivalent already includes the structural volume: Size 7 is 2 Spaces and Size 8 is 4. Removing arms, power or Slots never reduces this price basis. For current ordinary chassis the source 5% reservation is dominated by its one-Space minimum, so each complete parachute reserves 64 Slots. This is bounded accounting, not a general percentage conversion for future larger hosts. Smaller designs retain invalid whole-hardware fit. RH13 small-chassis table equivalents differ from RH14's twice-Base-Slots prose; positive printed equivalents remain the selected pricing basis, while Size 1's printed zero leaves price unresolved rather than creating free hardware. None of those small chassis can fit a parachute in this scope.

The four named parachute technologies retain their own introduction TL, rate, deployment altitude and supersonic eligibility at higher robot TL. No powered host is required, including for the grav chute. Deployment, atmosphere/gravity, clearance and interfaces remain contextual. Landing collision, critical hits and restoration cost are operating risks, not precharged construction damage. A used device requires whole-hardware replacement; no spare canopy, magazine, free reset, flight or automatic activation is granted.

UNREP records keep optional whole `allocatedSpaces` (default one only when omitted) separate from installation `quantity`. Allocated Spaces determine each device's cost, Slots and nominal transfer rate; quantity multiplies acquisition only and never pools device performance. Source minimum TL5 is availability metadata, not a claim that every system was manufactured at TL5. The application input bound is 1,000 allocated Spaces, not a source maximum. Selected power remains conditional, including external supply. Hoses and tubes are included; receiver compatibility, coupling and reach remain unverified. No cargo, fuel, ordnance, storage, recharge, crew or skills are granted. Returned editor facts and whole-component snapshots retain per-installation accounting; brain-only purchases exclude hardware and its operating warnings.

## Saved designs

Storage is isolated under `traveller.robot-builder.designs.v1`. No existing tool save is read, migrated or rewritten. Only explicit Save / Save a copy writes a named design. Import stages an unsaved draft and never overwrites saved designs.

Writes use an exclusive Web Lock plus expected prior bytes and post-write verification. Stale editors must refresh the saved list. A failed or uncertain write retains the draft for export and does not blindly retry. Corrupt or future-version storage blocks writes without deleting recoverable bytes. Browser support for Web Locks is required to save, but construction and export remain available without it. JSON backups are recommended.

## Component snapshots and augmentation scope

Composite records copy a selected saved or imported robot. They are not live links and never edit the original save. The engine revalidates each snapshot, propagates its diagnostics, derives fit and brain inputs, and accounts for each component once. Imported totals and verification flags are not trusted. Nested composites and incompatible construction estimates are rejected; component count and input size are bounded. A validated robot does not certify a ship hull, vehicle, biological host or operational radio link.

Cybernetic parts keep their own equipment, weapons, software and capacity. An implant's skills are not silently granted to its recipient. General-computer programs include the thirteen Robot Handbook pp122-123 creator and control tiers, alongside Core software. Active control programs require Intelligent Interface on the same computer; external links and targets remain unverified. Whole-body, limb-mounted and separate-servo construction remain distinct. Miscellaneous implants use source-defined Slot or mass admission, with the shared three-device limit. Core and supported CSC devices retain their own source labels, prices and operating conditions. Returned augmentation catalogues and evaluated device details are detached from the rules data. CSC medical and diagnostic devices retain per-subject calibration, consumable and contextual-use conditions rather than granting recipient skills or universal bonuses. The standard deconstruction chamber preserves per-chamber capacity, destructive operation and separate data storage; it does not manufacture objects or restore memories. Explicit source interpretations remain necessary where the book does not settle a price basis.

Direct payloads, associated launchers and stored individual rounds are distinct records. Seventy explicitly classified CSC profiles can allocate spare magazines or power packs to installed storage using the rule-of-thumb mass estimates on CSC p142. These estimates share the approximate two-kilogram-per-Slot storage budget with known-mass loose Core grenades. Included or separately purchased initial loads are not counted as spare cargo, linked copies are counted once each, and an autoloader does not also reserve compartment space. The ten-extra-load autoloader rule remains separate from the application's one-hundred-load stored-cargo input bound. Unknown pack locations, chemical containers, unpriced replacements and unsupported source families do not inherit an estimate. Estimates remain labelled rather than presented as measured masses; no storage choice certifies hazardous handling or automatic reloading.

Medical nanorobot records distinguish purchased active packages, purchased reserves and declared existing active packages. Costs apply only to purchases, while active counts and effects are combined across records. Physical enhancements retain explicit STR/DEX/END targets and mental enhancement uses INT; same-target records cannot evade the cumulative package requirement. Existing unknown package types, application outcomes and expiry remain qualified. Single-package designs still import, and switching to itemised records preserves the original selection without charging it twice. Anti-ageing reduction above effective TL15 is not specified: itemised effects remain blocked, while the old single-package extrapolation is explicitly labelled as a legacy assumption.

Delayed file reads carry a draft revision so an earlier import cannot overwrite a newer draft. Saving, importing and referencing a component remain separate actions.

Sixteen explicitly magazine-fed CSC configurations support separately selected standard/high-capacity initial loads and mixed spare quantities. The high-capacity accessory has its own TL4 gate and p175 capacity/price multipliers; its mass doubles the original p142 estimate once. Standard and high-capacity spares may use different compartments or a combined autoloader, with a shared ten-magazine autoload limit. Referee-dependent compatibility remains qualified, particularly shotguns, and other finite-capacity supplies do not automatically acquire this conversion. Optional fields preserve old backups; invalid retained choices stay visible and removable.

An optional explicit allocation can divide additional supplies between the autoloader and separately stored reserves. Existing quantities remain total extras; only the allocated subset attracts the doubled loader package price, and only the remainder reserves cargo space. The source limit is ten supplies per loader. The separate application bound is one hundred stored reserves per linked copy, allowing at most 110 total extras in explicit mode. Old designs omit the allocation object and retain their previous meaning and limits. Opening the allocation disclosure does not change a draft; selecting split mode preserves the existing effective allocation before editing.

Linked weapons can retain different initial loads, spare variants, loader allocations and storage assignments through optional complete per-copy specifications. Unlisted copies inherit the shared settings; customised copies do not change when shared quantities change. Hardware and fire control remain linked, with ammunition and capacity summed once per installed copy. Reducing the linked count retains out-of-range specifications for correction without counting removed copies. No averaged magazine capacity or per-weapon supply count is invented. Legacy designs omit the optional list and retain their previous meaning.

ARL standard/support magazine selections retain four distinct source products: each donor profile has ordinary and high-capacity variants. Initial loads and extra supplies retain their source prices, capacities and qualified mass estimates without changing the receiving weapon or its traits. Equal-capacity products are not interchangeable identities. All products share per-copy loader/reserve limits and compartment capacity. Donor-based mass is an explicit inventory convention, and cross-fitted high-capacity supplies remain referee-qualified. PDW interchange is not inferred. Optional source fields preserve legacy defaults and per-copy independence.

Remaining ammunition cases include included-pack replacement disposition and unverified high-capacity feed compatibility. Empty fitted-loader pricing and fitting already-owned supplies remain unmodelled. These are application or source boundaries, not claims that such constructions are prohibited.

## Held weapon grips

Supported held two-handed weapons use one weapon record with two distinct installed manipulators. Both grips participate in held-loadout occupancy checks; an arm-mounted attachment does not automatically occupy its gripping hand. A second grip never duplicates the weapon, ammunition, accessories or fire-control purchase. Missing, removed, shared or inapplicable references remain visible and repairable. Source-marked optional two-handed use is supported without inventing a damage benefit.

Actual arm characteristics are reported separately. Multi-arm effective characteristics and attack modifiers remain unresolved; no highest, averaged, summed or first-arm value is invented. Carrying fit, reach and coordinated operation remain referee-dependent, and archaic handedness remains a qualified physical interpretation. Held vehicle-class weapons and whole-recipient cyberlimb grip assignments remain outside this route. Mount-size prerequisites apply to attached mounts, not to held grips.

## External wearable weapons

Six source-specific non-powered CSC acquisitions (pp138–139) support hand or forearm association with an existing base or additional robot arm: Brass Knuckles, Handspikes, three non-powered Claw Gloves and Piston Fist. Each record buys one item. Mass printed as a dash remains unknown. The Piston Fist never adds positive or negative STR damage DM; no pneumatic refill, reset, reservoir or electrical rule is inferred. Claw deployment and attack resolution are not simulated.

External wear requires the explicit per-design convention `external-wear-no-internal-slots-v1`. It is an optional referee accounting assumption, not a published construction rule: ordinary single-item purchase price, no integral weapon mount and no internal Slot reservation. It is not the miscellaneous-implant route, does not consume a Zero-Slot allowance, and does not imply zero mass or volume. Fit and wear/grip/mount overlaps remain unresolved referee conditions. The setting is absent from legacy designs and is not selected by adding hardware. Unselected or unknown versions retain acquisition facts but block installed-wear accounting. Unsupported retained settings remain visible and removable.

Worn purchase prices are outside body multipliers and the ordinary chassis minimum-cost floor. Whole-component quantities scale their own purchases once; brain-only acquisitions exclude worn hardware and its physical-fit warnings. Design results, review, print and detached component snapshots preserve both wear and any metamorphic provenance. Converted-leg/foot/shoulder wear, recipient/cyberlimb copies, powered or stun gear, linked weapons, fire control, accessories and ammunition customization are outside this bounded route. Existing held-grip conflicts and ordinary mounted accounting are unchanged.

## Microbot software and high-tech nanorobots

Microbots use the shared hard-coded package and standard software validation. Basic (none) can install one compatible package up to level one; no Bandwidth module is available. Hard-coded weapon and servant choices are explicit, and Basic (locomotion) retains level-zero movement skills without an invented agility enhancement. Custom software retains normal prices, inherent Bandwidth limits and characteristic modifiers. Protection multipliers apply to hardware, not software. Old backups without the new selectors retain defaults; a missing weapon specialty remains unresolved rather than becoming a generic weapon skill.

Brainless microbots validate receiver and communications across both legacy hardware and separate installations. Higher-technology nanoconstructors expose their existing external controller capacity input. This route continues to treat the selected construction bags as operated together; reserve inventory and scheduling are not modelled. High-tech nanorobot protections follow the general Handbook multipliers while retaining each product's printed litre or package price basis. Unknown working life, production rate, intelligence and Bandwidth remain unknown.

## Rules presentation

The editor, design review and printable statblock show concise labels, selected figures and source references. Full reference rows and long rule explanations are not displayed. Estimated values, incomplete coverage, unresolved results, source interpretations and referee-dependent conditions remain labelled; corrective checks identify the relevant part, weapon and linked copy. Detailed engine diagnostics remain available for verification without becoming presentation text. User-entered design names and specialties are retained.

## Clone development

Clones use biological templates, growth services and learned skills rather than a robot chassis/brain conversion. Accelerated growth can stop at the convenient whole-month choices or a bounded custom fractional month. Source-dependent pricing, partial-year education and calendar interpretations are recorded explicitly; no prorated fee or premature completed-year training grant is invented.

Timed INT purchases preserve cumulative costs, point banking and annual EDU progression. Actual post-maturity training advances the ageing clock, and uncredited partial creche months still count toward the sixteen-year limit. Required ageing rolls are not manufactured: affected designs retain a provisional calendar and ageing obligations while current-character certification is blocked. The printed thirty-three-versus-thirty-four-year calendar conflict remains visible. Natural-grown clone backgrounds and immature characteristics require their appropriate biological or character records.

Eleven powered CSC melee configurations separate complete hardware acquisition from current charge and replacement supplies. Printed weapon durations and recharge rules remain unchanged. Where the source omits endurance, the weapon follows actual robot endurance under an explicitly labelled user/referee ruling; an unknown host or biological/recipient endurance does not become unlimited power or END-hours. Unsupported replacement purchases, loaders and physical power connections remain unavailable. Melee (whip) is a distinct CSC specialty, while original Core all-listed software keeps its four-specialty price and Bandwidth. Explicit purchased specialty coverage now reaches weapon ratings and permits non-overlapping additional specialties.

Four additional CSC melee acquisitions cover Monofilament Axe, Spear, Lance and Piston Spear. Source-qualified grips determine mounts independently of mass. Lance and Piston Spear retain unresolved typed attack modes, conditional damage headlines and an exact ordinary-Spear fallback without changing purchased hardware. Lance requires riding a fast-moving animal or vehicle; robot speed or a weapon mount does not establish that condition. Piston Spear requires a Minor Action reset after every attack without an invented reservoir count or supply price. Spear's TL0 fragility depends on unknown manufacturing TL, not robot TL, and does not transfer to Lance or Piston hardware. No combat-state simulation or automatic Javelin profile is introduced.
## Provisional metamorphic construction

Metamorphic (provisional) is a separate whole-robot construction kind, not an android or cyborg conversion. Robot Handbook p104 supplies the three immutable tier TLs, rates, transformation times and appearance levels. Construction uses the explicit versioned application convention `provisional-base-slots-standard-v1`: tier rate times original chassis Base Slots, added to ordinary chassis/equipment costs; zero additional body-reserved Slots; no android multiplier; ordinary current-form manipulator Size, STR, DEX, fit and pricing. Ordinary armour, power, brain and capacity checks remain in force. High TL alone does not require sentience.

The fixed-zero reservation and all formula choices are provisional assumptions, not printed construction definitions or full-book certification. Removal of unused Slots never lowers the metamorphic rate basis. Current form is bounded by the standard manipulator controls; Size and locomotion stay fixed within morphing, and features do not grant free equipment, attacks or software. Transient forms and operating combat are not simulated. Review, statblock, API, JSON convention identity and supported whole-robot component snapshots retain provisional provenance. Invalid TL and capacity selections stay available for correction. Brain-only vehicle integration projects ordinary brain/upgrades/software prices without buying the source metamorphic body; its provisional source provenance stays explicit. Organic-brain conversion remains distinct and retains its existing guard.

## Reference sheet
Choose **Reference sheet** under Design files to preview a compact ruled sheet, then use **Print reference sheet** (or the browser's Print command) to print or save a PDF. **Detailed statblock** remains the default and preserves its existing full output. The display-only choice is not saved in design JSON or browser design records. No sample artwork or branding is bundled.

The API's read-only `getSheet()` uses evaluated totals and qualified review facts through `js/sheet-model.mjs`; the text-only DOM renderer in `js/sheet.mjs` performs no rules arithmetic. Missing values remain unresolved, preview totals retain their status, and attached component/implant records never become host or recipient grants. This is a quick reference: use the detailed statblock for full installation settings, conditional attack modes, source details and component profiles. Long rows and checks may span pages instead of being clipped or scaled down.

## Bounded CSC specialised ammunition

Five single modifications (Armour Piercing, Solid Shot, APDS, Baton and Extended Range) are supported for fifteen explicitly classified CSC slug profiles and the two CSC standard-ammunition launchers. The current capability registry is returned by the API. Core identities, other CSC profiles and ordinary inventory paths remain separate. Fourteen further verified heavy exceptions are deferred implementation scope, not a prohibition. Other round modifications, combinations, loose-round economy, higher/lower manufacture products and extra high-capacity eligibility are not inferred.

A selected manufacture TL is a user design declaration covering all linked copies. Legacy records leave actual manufacture unknown. Certification requires that declaration to equal the selected catalogue profile TL and meet the ammunition threshold, independently of robot TL. Higher/lower declarations remain visible and unresolved, without clamping or repricing hardware. This is an application support boundary, not a source ban on later manufacture or a referee approval requirement for an ordinary catalogue-tier declaration.

Modification identity is independent of magazine source and capacity variant. Initial loads, spare fields and additional product rows may hold different single modifications. Complete per-copy settings preserve shared/custom isolation. Source magazine price is multiplied once by the capacity-price factor and ammunition multiplier; allocated loader supplies alone enter the doubled RH p61 loader package. One shared ten-physical-magazine loader limit and the existing application reserve bound apply. Estimates retain the standard-load CSC p142 basis and p175 high-capacity multiplier; actual modified-load mass is unresolved. Cost multipliers never multiply mass.

Modified shotgun loads do not inherit pellet/spread benefits. Fractional ranges remain exact; sight thresholds and stock range bands remain independent. These facts do not simulate combat.

The editor, review, component/limb projections and actual Print statblock use selected labels and figures, source references and unresolved-state labels. JSON/import/save preserve optional fields and retained unavailable identities. The source text and private audit remain outside Git.
