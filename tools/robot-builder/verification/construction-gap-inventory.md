# Robot Builder construction boundaries and release work

Scope: Robot Handbook (Mongoose 2022), broad ordinary and special new-build construction. This inventory separates finite release work from source omissions, referee choices, external records and optional later workflows. Neither catalogue rows nor representative examples certify every possible legal robot.

The final Handbook corrections are implemented. Independent source review and the full syntax, native and browser gates on the final candidate remain pending; this document does not close those gates.

Athletics limb equivalents and weapon/equipment readiness use the purchased scope rather than an unrelated positive speciality. - **Brainless microbot installations (RH 79-81).** Drone-interface and communication checks use combined legacy and independent equipment instances. Real receiver/link prerequisites and no-brain software restrictions remain.
- **High-tech constructor controls (RH 84-85,105).** Enhanced and advanced constructors expose controller task capacity. The check concerns simultaneous deployment, not a guarantee that purchased reserve inventory can operate together.
- **Optional broad software (RH 73).** Explicit referee four- and eight-purchase choices support approved full breadth, including open/custom Art and Profession specialities. Existing individual, all-listed and finite legacy selections retain their price, Bandwidth and saved meaning; no legacy selection silently becomes universal.
- **High-tech nano protection (RH 81,83,105).** General environmental multipliers apply to acquisition in its source unit: per litre for high-tech constructors and per package for distributed consciousness, with cumulative radiation pricing. Missing operating ratings remain unknown.

These corrections have focused regression suites and two dedicated browser runners. Their existence is not evidence that the final merged candidate has passed review or aggregate checks.

## Source ambiguities and qualified constructions

- **Aeroplane movement (RH 17,23,216):** vehicle movement and a minimum Slow band are required, but p23 omits a base band. SkySpotter's printed Medium is example evidence, not a general erratum. Aeroplane mapping and primary-thruster edge cases remain qualified; ordinary stationary plus secondary-thruster construction is a separate supported route.
- **Microbot exceptions (RH 79-81):** micro DEX upgrade price, aeroplane/primary-thruster reconciliation, the Advanced microbrain interpretation and its retrotech fit issue remain source-dependent. Sub-milligram input does not establish an unprinted minimum mass. Software parity does not resolve those hardware ambiguities.
- **Medical ageing reduction (RH 83-84):** acquisition and lifetime above TL15 are defined; reduction above effective TL15 is not. Itemised effects retain that boundary. The old single-package 90% extrapolation remains a labelled legacy assumption.
- **Neural computers and implants (RH 67,89,92-94):** the advanced-comm Computer/1-to-/2 upgrade has a printed price; the basic-link Computer/0-to-/2 route does not. Ordinary brain upgrades are implemented. Cybernetic software surcharges and implant retrotech pricing retain separate explicit interpretations, as do limb increase prices and master-interface DEX.
- **High technology (RH 104-105,113):** continuous-backup implant fit, distributed-consciousness INT/Bandwidth, high-tech constructor lifetime/throughput and open-fabrication output bounds remain unspecified. Source prices support acquisitions, not complete operating brains or facilities. Superior-avatar reserved Slots require their disclosed interpretation.
- **Metamorphic bodies (RH 104):** the approved provisional current-form convention uses immutable Base Slots, ordinary hardware prices, zero extra body reservation and standard manipulator limits. These are versioned application assumptions, not printed definitions. Arbitrary free limbs and transient forms are outside scope; missing definitions do not reopen this approved convention as a release blocker.
- **Power and software context (RH 33,55-57,69-73):** Hunter/Killer Bandwidth, short-RTG price and solar-coating STR/Agility interpretations remain explicit. Mixed degraded sources, dark charging history and stationary useful STR remain unresolved operating claims; no wattage model or automatic alternative-bonus sum is invented.
- **Weapon fit and supplies (RH 60-64; Core 126,135; CSC142,175):** combined rifle/Heavy Weapons mounting, held vehicle-class weapons, accessory mass at the 250kg boundary, unknown charge/container masses and unpriced replacements retain specific limits. Spare-load estimates are not universal measured masses. Included-pack disposition, empty fitted loaders and already-owned supply economics remain unmodelled.

A selected construction must retain its specific limitation. Passing tests cannot supply a missing rule, recipient record or referee choice.

## External records and representative examples

Vehicle/ship hull legality, available host capacity and target links remain external even when robot/brain snapshots revalidate. High Guard Update 2022 pp 36-37 supplies long-range case procurement, not an individual long-range missile price or every carrier/payload variant. Its inspected revised printing is copyright 2026. VH Update 2026 is the explicitly supported vehicle source; locating an older edition is not a release prerequisite.

Biological templates, unusual anatomy, genetic changes, recipient skills, donor data and treatment compatibility require appropriate records (RH 86-99). Adult template characteristics do not certify immature clones. The clone age 33/34 discrepancy, partial-year education/pricing choices and unresolved ageing outcomes remain explicit. A genome flag does not replace a species or character generator.

Preserve the [published comparison differences](published-validation.md): Advanced Courier's one-Slot overrun and 5-versus-12-hour vehicle endurance; Zombie's four rather than three spare Slots; StarTek's separately required initial pack; Nano Hero's price difference; and Sabmiqys's one-Slot overrun. RH 76 permits catalogue prices to differ from construction costs. Do not alter source inputs to force matches. Published reconstructions and focused implant/computer tests are representative evidence only.

CSC Update 2023 devices, weapons, accessories and ammunition use the revised copyright 2026 printing with source-specific IDs, not silent replacements for Core/Handbook rows. Additional CSC, VH or High Guard catalogues are optional later work; no full external-book completion gate is imposed.

## Final release gates and separate workflows

1. Complete independent source review and any concrete correction it identifies, including component skill projections. Keep legacy saves and explicit conventions intact.
2. Run the configured syntax/native aggregate and all browser suites on the exact final candidate. Distinguish passed, failed and unrun checks; earlier baseline results do not certify a merged head.
3. Exercise ordinary, corrected microbot, medical/high-tech nano and provisional/host-dependent designs at desktop and narrow widths. Cover repair of retained-invalid choices, type/TL changes, six stages, Save/Save a copy/reload, JSON import/export and the actual Print statblock output. Check stale-tab, unavailable-lock, rejected/corrupt/delayed-import and text-escaping regressions without real campaign data.
4. Confirm beta version, changelog, scope labels and shared-site links. Merge/deployment require the final gates; live route/assets, save/export/print and responsive checks follow authorised publication.

Combat, surgery/treatment outcomes, charging/refill history, damage/maintenance, retrofit histories, factory/fleet operation, a full support-procurement inventory and complete character careers remain separate workflows. A selected Robot-as-Traveller output needs its own scoped projection; ordinary construction does not imply character legality.
