# Catalogue test summary

These are historical test results, not verified vehicle designs or official errata. The 99 catalogue test designs and detailed vehicle data were removed from the current publication on October 3, 2026. Reports retain aggregate findings only; they contain no vehicle designs, source stat blocks or design downloads.

## Mechanical comparison
- 99 non-biological vehicle drafts were compared.
- 5 matched the checked mechanical values as labelled.
- 5 additional drafts matched after an explicitly inferred Hull/Structure label correction.
- 89 had differing mechanics, invalid allocations or unresolved details.
- Price differences were excluded from the mechanical comparison.
- A mismatch does not establish that a published vehicle is impossible or incorrect.

## Hull and Structure
- 43 printed values matched calculated Structure.
- 54 printed values instead matched calculated Hull.
- 2 matched neither.
- These counts overlap the mechanical results above; they are not additional vehicles.
- Label corrections were interpretations, not official errata.

## Component mapping
- 1,885 component occurrences were reviewed.
- 1,861 were mapped to builder options.
- 24 remained unresolved.
- Mapping does not establish that every operating rule is implemented or every value independently verified.

## Limits
These results describe an earlier testing run. They cannot be rerun from this publication because the catalogue fixtures have been removed. Current construction and interface tests remain available in source/. The equipment reference tables required by the builder are separate from these removed vehicle designs.
