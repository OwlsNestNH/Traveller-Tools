Crew and condition

Quick values and overrides are TOTAL crew contributions; no characteristic DM is added again. Detailed contributions are skill + characteristic DM. These are user-selected setup conventions; no checks are rolled.

Every ship moves. Unplotted ships coast with their existing velocity. There is no facing rule in this prototype.

What this alpha does — and does not

Try persistent velocity, per-ship thrust, reachable hexes, plotted movement, round advance, and a proportional map. Range labels are abstract hex-step distances from the selected ship; they are not verified range bands or kilometres.

The engine applies a chosen velocity change, moves using the updated velocity, then clears that round’s thrust. That integration order and the thrust-to-hex conversion are prototype assumptions. Precise Mongoose Traveller 2e integration, physical scale, reaction-engine performance and mixed six-minute/six-second combat remain unresolved.

No weapons firing, damage resolution, missiles, dogfights or campaign import. Ship and crew setup does not resolve checks. The catalogue retains only minimum setup facts; unlisted armour stays unknown rather than inferred. Source thrust is used as a prototype budget under the assumptions above.
